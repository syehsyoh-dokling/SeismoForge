# Project brief: Valley Office Tower

A developer wants an eight-story commercial office building in a valley city
with moderate-to-high seismicity on firm alluvium. Tenant comfort and
insurance terms favour an isolated solution if it is economical; the frame
itself is a conventional stiff core.

## Project data

- Building use: office
- Stories above grade: 8
- Seismic floor weight: 600 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 380,000 kN/m
- Story height: 3.4 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.25 g
- Predominant site period: 0.7 s
- Strong-motion duration: 22 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.55 m
