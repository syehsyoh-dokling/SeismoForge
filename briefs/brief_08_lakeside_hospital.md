# Project brief: Lakeside Community Hospital

A three-story community hospital near a lake shore, on medium-stiff soils in
a moderate zone. Operational continuity drives the brief: imaging and surgical
equipment set a hard ceiling on floor accelerations.

## Project data

- Building use: hospital
- Stories above grade: 3
- Seismic floor weight: 500 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 420,000 kN/m
- Story height: 3.6 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.22 g
- Predominant site period: 0.8 s
- Strong-motion duration: 22 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.55 m
