# Project brief: Hillside Distribution Warehouse

A logistics operator needs a simple two-story racking warehouse in a
low-to-moderate seismic region on stiff hillside soils. Contents are
palletized goods; the owner wants the cheapest system that verifiably meets
the performance targets, and suspects isolation is unnecessary here.

## Project data

- Building use: warehouse
- Stories above grade: 2
- Seismic floor weight: 400 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 150,000 kN/m
- Story height: 4.5 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.15 g
- Predominant site period: 0.5 s
- Strong-motion duration: 20 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.35 m
