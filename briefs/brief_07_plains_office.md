# Project brief: Plains Office Campus Block

A six-story office block on firm plains soils in a moderate seismic zone. The
client's engineer believes a conventional fixed-base frame is sufficient here
and wants that assumption tested honestly before spending on bearings.

## Project data

- Building use: office
- Stories above grade: 6
- Seismic floor weight: 580 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 320,000 kN/m
- Story height: 3.5 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.18 g
- Predominant site period: 0.6 s
- Strong-motion duration: 20 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.45 m
