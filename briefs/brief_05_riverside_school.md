# Project brief: Riverside School

A school district is replacing a four-story classroom block on river-deposit
soils. Life safety plus post-earthquake usability as a neighbourhood shelter
are the goals; the district asked explicitly for evidence that the chosen
system holds its targets across multiple plausible ground motions, not just
one design record.

## Project data

- Building use: school
- Stories above grade: 4
- Seismic floor weight: 480 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 300,000 kN/m
- Story height: 3.5 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.30 g
- Predominant site period: 1.0 s
- Strong-motion duration: 24 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.60 m
