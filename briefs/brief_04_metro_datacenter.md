# Project brief: Metro Data Center

A colocation provider is building a three-story data hall near a metropolitan
fault system. Rack equipment is acceleration-sensitive and downtime is
contractual money, so floor accelerations dominate the design conversation.
The floor plates are heavy with mechanical plant.

## Project data

- Building use: data_center
- Stories above grade: 3
- Seismic floor weight: 800 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 500,000 kN/m
- Story height: 4.0 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.28 g
- Predominant site period: 0.9 s
- Strong-motion duration: 24 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.60 m
