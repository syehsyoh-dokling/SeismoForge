# Project brief: Downtown Residential Tower

A twelve-story apartment tower on a constrained downtown lot. The structural
system is a stiff wall-frame; the lot line limits how much isolator travel the
podium can accommodate. The owner wants to know whether isolation still pays
off with that constraint.

## Project data

- Building use: residential
- Stories above grade: 12
- Seismic floor weight: 650 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 550,000 kN/m
- Story height: 3.0 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.26 g
- Predominant site period: 0.8 s
- Strong-motion duration: 22 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.50 m
