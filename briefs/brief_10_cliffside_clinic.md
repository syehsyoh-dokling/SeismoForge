# Project brief: Cliffside Clinic

A six-story clinic proposed on a severe near-fault site with very soft, long-
period soils, on a lot whose retaining condition caps the moat at 0.40 m. The
client is aware the site is aggressive and wants a straight answer: is there a
buildable isolation design that meets hospital-grade performance here, or does
the project need a different site, a bigger moat, or supplemental damping
outside our standard system?

## Project data

- Building use: hospital
- Stories above grade: 6
- Seismic floor weight: 700 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 400,000 kN/m
- Story height: 3.6 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.38 g
- Predominant site period: 1.3 s
- Strong-motion duration: 28 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.40 m
