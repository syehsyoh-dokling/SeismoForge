# Project brief: Coastal Hospital

A regional health authority is planning a five-story acute-care hospital on a
reclaimed coastal site with deep soft soil. The facility must remain
operational immediately after a major earthquake, so equipment accelerations
and structural drift are both tightly controlled. The structural concept is a
stiff concrete shear-wall block; the question for us is the base isolation
layer and whether the available moat is enough on this soil.

## Project data

- Building use: hospital
- Stories above grade: 5
- Seismic floor weight: 550 tonnes per floor
- Story lateral stiffness: 450,000 kN/m
- Story height: 3.6 m

## Site hazard

- Design PGA: 0.32 g
- Predominant site period: 1.1 s
- Strong-motion duration: 25 s
- Moat clearance available: 0.90 m
