## Engineering notes

A lead-rubber base-isolated scheme was selected for the 5-story hospital because the fixed-base period estimate is near the site predominant period and the hospital acceleration limit is stringent. The verified design is governed by isolator travel, with peak displacement below the 0.9 m moat clearance; residual displacement is the next closest check. The adopted 4.0 s-class isolation stiffness trades a larger but acceptable moat demand for reduced transmitted force and floor acceleration.
