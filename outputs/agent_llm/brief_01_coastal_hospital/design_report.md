## Engineering notes

A lead-rubber base-isolated scheme is recommended for this stiff five-storey hospital on soft coastal soil. The initial isolation size controlled accelerations poorly; moving to a longer-period isolation layer reduced floor acceleration and base shear, then modestly increasing characteristic strength recovered moat clearance. The governing check is isolator displacement: the simulated peak travel is 0.890 m against the 0.900 m available, so detailed design should preserve the full moat, control construction tolerances, and consider whether any additional clearance can be created. Base shear and acceleration remain within the operational targets with moderate margins.
