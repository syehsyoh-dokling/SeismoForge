## Engineering notes

The fixed-base frame was checked first because the client asked whether the frame alone is adequate; it does not pass the simulated suite, with floor acceleration and base shear slightly above the brief limits. A lead-rubber base-isolated scheme tuned to about a 3.2 s isolated period was therefore selected. The governing check for the selected scheme is isolator displacement, leaving roughly 0.136 m of the 0.45 m moat clearance unused; force, drift, acceleration, and recentring checks all retain wider margins. The chosen strength level trades a modest increase in transmitted acceleration for substantially better moat margin than the lowest-strength isolation options.
