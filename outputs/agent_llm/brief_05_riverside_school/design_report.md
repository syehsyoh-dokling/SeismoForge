## Engineering notes

Lead-rubber base isolation is justified for the school: the fixed-base period estimate is close to the 1 s soil period, while the selected approximately 4 s isolation stiffness moves the structure away from site resonance and keeps the superstructure drifts and accelerations within the school criteria. The controlling item in the verified nonlinear suite is moat displacement, with 0.528 m peak travel against 0.600 m available; the trade was to use moderate characteristic strength for recentering and travel control without driving floor acceleration above the 0.45 g target.
