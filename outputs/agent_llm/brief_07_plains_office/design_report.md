## Engineering notes

The fixed-base rule-of-thumb option was simulated first because the hazard is low-to-moderate and the client is cost-sensitive; it narrowly missed the floor-acceleration criterion, with peak floor acceleration 0.574 g versus the 0.55 g limit, while drift and base shear passed. A modest lead-rubber isolation scheme was therefore selected. In the verification simulation it kept isolator displacement to 0.201 m within the 0.45 m moat, peak drift to 0.00541 versus 0.012, peak floor acceleration to 0.289 g versus 0.55, and base shear coefficient to 0.191 versus 0.45. The governing check is floor acceleration at utilization about 0.526, so the design has substantial response margin; the trade is adding isolation to satisfy the acceleration target rather than accepting the near-miss fixed-base building.
