## Engineering notes

A lead-rubber base-isolated scheme is recommended because the data-center acceleration limit governs the concept. The initial isolation sizing missed the floor-acceleration cap; screening showed the design space trade-off between low acceleration and moat travel, and the selected refinement raises characteristic strength enough to control displacement while keeping transmitted acceleration below the 0.30 g tenant limit. Governing demand is isolator travel, with usable moat margin remaining; drift and base shear are comfortably below their limits.
