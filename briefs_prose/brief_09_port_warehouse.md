# Port logistics shed

Three-storey logistics shed inside the port estate. Bulk storage, no fragile
contents, but the ground is reclaimed fill so the shaking is not trivial.

Seismic weight per floor is 450 tonnes. Storey lateral stiffness is 200 MN/m
and the storey height is 4.2 m.

Hazard: design PGA 0.24 g, predominant site period 0.9 s, strong-motion
duration 23 s. Moat clearance available is 0.45 m.

Let us know whether the frame alone is adequate.
