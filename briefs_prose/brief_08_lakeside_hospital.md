# Lakeside hospital annex

Three-storey hospital annex beside the lake. It houses imaging and diagnostics,
so it is subject to the same continuity-of-operation targets as the main
hospital block.

Each floor is 500 tonnes of seismic weight; storey lateral stiffness is
420,000 kN/m; floor-to-floor 3.6 m.

The site is a moderate lakeside deposit: design PGA 0.22 g, predominant site
period 0.8 s, 22 s of strong motion. We have 0.55 m of clearance available
for a moat.

Please confirm the protection strategy.
