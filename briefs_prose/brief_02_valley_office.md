# Valley office block

Following up on the valley office scheme. Eight storeys above grade, ordinary
commercial occupancy - open-plan offices, nothing unusual in the fit-out.

Each floor comes in at 600 tonnes of seismic weight and the frame gives us
about 380,000 kN/m of lateral stiffness per storey. Floor-to-floor is 3.4 m.

The site is a firm valley-floor deposit: design PGA 0.25 g, predominant site
period 0.7 s, strong-motion duration 22 s. We have 0.55 m of moat clearance
to play with.

Question for you: is isolation worth it here, or does a conventional frame
carry it?
