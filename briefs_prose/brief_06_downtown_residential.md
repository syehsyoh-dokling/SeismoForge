# Downtown residential tower

Twelve storeys of residential apartments in the downtown block. Nothing
special about the occupancy - homes, normal contents.

Per floor: 650 tonnes seismic weight, 550,000 kN/m storey lateral stiffness,
3 m floor to floor.

The site sits on medium-stiff ground. Design PGA is 0.26 g, predominant site
period 0.8 s, strong-motion duration 22 s. Neighbouring plots are tight - we
can only offer 0.5 m of moat clearance.

Would like your view on whether the moat is the binding constraint.
