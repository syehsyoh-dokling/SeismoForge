# Riverside school replacement

Concept advice needed on a four-storey school building on the river terrace -
soft alluvium, unfortunately.

Loading: 480 tonnes seismic weight per floor, storey lateral stiffness
300,000 kN/m, storey height 3.5 m.

The seismologist's numbers are a design PGA of 0.3 g, a predominant site
period of 1 s, and 24 s of strong motion. Site constraints leave 0.6 m of
moat clearance.

Occupancy is a school, so the acceptance targets sit between an office and a
hospital. Is isolation justified?
