# Hillside distribution warehouse

Simple one for you. Two-storey warehouse on a hillside site, stiff shallow
rock, used for palletised storage only - no sensitive contents.

Seismic weight is 400 tonnes per floor. Storey lateral stiffness is on the
low side at 150,000 kN/m, and the clear height between floors is 4.5 m
because of the racking.

Hazard is mild: design PGA 0.15 g, predominant site period 0.5 s, and about
20 s of strong motion. Available clearance around the footprint is 0.35 m.

I suspect this does not need isolation at all but the client wants it checked.
