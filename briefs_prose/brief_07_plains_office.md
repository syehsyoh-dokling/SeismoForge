# Plains office - six storeys

Straightforward six-storey office on flat plains ground, firm soil profile.

Structure: 580 tonnes of seismic weight on each floor, storey lateral
stiffness 320,000 kN/m, storey height 3.5 m.

Hazard is low-to-moderate - design PGA 0.18 g, predominant site period 0.6 s,
and strong motion lasting about 20 s. Clearance available around the building
is 0.45 m.

Client is cost-sensitive so please say plainly if isolation is unnecessary.
