# Cliffside clinic - feasibility check

This one worries me and I would like a second opinion before we commit.

Six-storey clinic on a cliffside parcel. It is an acute-care facility, so the
hospital performance targets apply. Each floor carries 700 tonnes of seismic
weight, storey lateral stiffness is 400,000 kN/m, and floor-to-floor is 3.6 m.

The site is the problem. We are close to the fault trace on soft ground: the
design PGA comes out at 0.38 g, the predominant site period is 1.3 s, and the
strong-motion duration is 28 s. Worse, the parcel boundary leaves us only
0.4 m of moat clearance.

If this cannot be built to the brief as posed, say so - the client can still
revisit the parcel.
