# Metro colocation facility - structural concept

We are designing a three-storey data hall in the metro area. The tenant's
equipment tolerances are strict, so floor accelerations matter more here than
anything else.

Each floor carries 7,848 kN of seismic weight - that is the raised-floor
build-up plus racks. Lateral stiffness per storey is 500,000 kN/m and the
floor-to-floor dimension is 4 m to clear the plenum.

Site data: design PGA of 0.28 g, predominant site period 0.9 s, strong-motion
duration 24 s. The plot gives us 0.6 m of clearance for any isolation moat.

Please advise on the protection concept.
