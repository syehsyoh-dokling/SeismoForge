# Coastal hospital - concept enquiry

Hi - we are at concept stage on a regional acute-care hospital and would like
a view on whether it needs a base isolation layer.

The building is five storeys above grade on reclaimed coastal ground, deep
soft soil. Structurally it is a stiff concrete shear-wall block: each storey
carries about 550 tonnes of seismic weight, the lateral stiffness works out at
roughly 450,000 kN/m per storey, and floor-to-floor is 3.6 m.

On the hazard side our consultant gives a design PGA of 0.32 g with a
predominant site period of about 1.1 s, and expects strong shaking to last
around 25 s. The site plan leaves us 0.9 m of clearance around the building
before we hit the retaining line.

The facility has to stay operational straight after a major event, so both
equipment accelerations and drift are tightly controlled. What would you
recommend?
